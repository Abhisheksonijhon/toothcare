Braces and clear aligners in Panna

Straight teeth are easier to clean and bite together more evenly. We offer metal braces, tooth-coloured ceramic braces and clear aligners for children, teenagers and adults.

Book a consultation 075099 99033

Who this helps

  • Crowded, crooked or overlapping teeth
  • Gaps between teeth
  • Teeth that stick out, or an upper and lower bite that do not meet properly
  • Adults who want to straighten teeth with less visible treatment

What happens, step by step

  1. Assessment

    Examination, X-rays and photographs show what is moving and why.

  2. Choosing the type

    Metal braces, ceramic braces or clear aligners, based on your bite, age and budget.

  3. Regular adjustments

    Visits every few weeks keep treatment on track.

  4. Retainers

    After braces, retainers hold teeth in their new position. Skipping them is the main reason teeth drift back.

What to expect

Most treatment takes somewhere between one and two years, though it varies with the case. Clear aligners work only if they are worn for the recommended hours each day, so we are honest about whether they suit you.

Frequently asked questions

Can adults get braces?

Yes. Healthy teeth and gums can be moved at any age. Many of our braces patients are adults.

Do braces hurt?

Teeth feel sore for a few days after fitting and after adjustments. Soft food and simple pain relief usually help.

Do I need a retainer?

Yes. Teeth naturally try to return to their old position, so retainers are an important part of treatment.
